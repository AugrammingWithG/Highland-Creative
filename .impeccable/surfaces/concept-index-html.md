## Direction contract

THESIS: Highland Creative's forty years as a living Southern Highlands show day: a 3D world where their own tree turns through the seasons on the hill, the class ribbons ripple in the field, and the work hangs in a pavilion you can walk past and spin. It refuses the static category page: nothing on this site sits still.

OWN-WORLD: One persistent, realistic WebGL scene of the Southern Highlands. Physically based materials, ACES tone mapping, soft sun shadows and atmospheric haze. A real deciduous tree (bark-textured tapered trunk, thousands of leaves) turns through the seasons on a pasture of wind-blown grass. Painted tree impostors stand on the far hills under a procedural cumulus sky. Show-day objects are rendered as materials: satin class ribbons, a striped marquee over timber boards, a satin rosette and a weatherboard studio with a plum corrugated roof. Plum card-stock and manila tags float over it as touchable DOM objects. Bevan wood type, Sofia Sans Extra Condensed caps labels, Sofia Sans body.

STORY: The visitor arrives on a living hill and drags the tree. Scrolling turns the year, then the camera flies through the classes field, the sky, the pavilion of real entries and the judges' rosette to the hill at dusk. They believe the studio because the work and the clients' words are real, and they lodge the swinging entry tag or call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed 3D hill and tree, with the tree right of centre, swaying and reacting to the cursor and draggable to orbit. A plum schedule cover card on the left holds the headline "Graphic & web design that works.", the lede, actions and season controls. At top right a manila entry tag hangs on a physics string and swings when touched, holding the working form. The header strip is live and tap-to-call.

FORM: The Highlands Show Schedule (seed key fdfdcad1), rebuilt as a continuous 3D world under the user's standing rule. Signature interaction: scroll turns the seasons (falling autumn leaves, winter snow), then camera-flight transitions between stations and between pages. Touch physics on tags and cards. A reduced-motion path renders a still scene.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
