Five quick wins we noticed along the way

Each one is small, and together they take under two hours. We’re happy to fix them for you at no cost, as a way to show how we work.

  1. The call button dials a different number

    What we found
    The homepage shows 02 4872 4490, but its tap-to-call link is tel:1300486931.
    Why it matters
    On phones, people tap the number. They may be reaching a line you don’t answer.
    The fix
    Point the link at tel:+61248724490.

    Time to fix5 min

  2. A test popup is live on every page

    What we found
    A popup titled Test Event Registration is in the page code site-wide.
    Why it matters
    Anyone who triggers it sees test content, and screen readers can announce it.
    The fix
    Remove the test popup or its shortcode.

    Time to fix10 min

  3. The footer still says 2018

    What we found
    Every page ends with Copyright © 2018.
    Why it matters
    Visitors read an old year as a business that’s gone quiet, which you haven’t.
    The fix
    Make the year update itself automatically.

    Time to fix5 min

  4. No social links or public reviews

    What we found
    No social links on the site, and we couldn’t find public reviews or a Google Business Profile.
    Why it matters
    Reviews are one of the first things local buyers check, and your clients already say great things.
    The fix
    Claim the profile, invite a few happy clients to review, and link it all from the footer.

    Time to fix1–2 hrs

  5. Pinch-to-zoom is switched off

    What we found
    The page settings include user-scalable=no.
    Why it matters
    People with low vision can’t zoom in on phones. It also fails an accessibility guideline (WCAG 1.4.4).
    The fix
    Remove that one setting.

    Time to fix5 min

One more small thing: the team page mentions “over 27 years” of experience, but the studio started in 1985. That’s 41 years, and worth saying proudly.
