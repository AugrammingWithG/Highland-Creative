Our story

Highly qualified designers and design educators, we offer a complete graphic design, web design and copywriting service to suit most client requirements.

We work closely with clients to identify the essence of each business and develop appealing, appropriate and creative bespoke designs. Our personal approach and our reputation for delivering strong, innovative work on time have built long-term relationships with our clients.

Since 1985 the company has grown from a sole operator providing hand-drawn graphic design and typesetting into a computerised design studio supporting a hand-picked team of creatives and technicians.

Our clients range widely in size and business: from engineers to artists, financiers to stock breeders, real estate agents to wineries, and council to corporate to quilters, from large manufacturers to sole operators.
