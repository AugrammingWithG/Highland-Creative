Questions committees ask

What do I need to sign up?

Wild Apricot offers a free trial with just an email address and no credit card. Payment details are only needed when you upgrade to a paid subscription.

What do I get with the free trial?

The trial includes the features of the Community plan for 30 days. If you don’t upgrade, the account switches to the free, ad-supported plan, so you keep your data.

Are there transaction fees?

Wild Apricot’s plans are flat-rate: no setup, customisation, training, per-transaction or cancellation fees. You only pay your payment provider’s fees if you take payments online.

Do you offer setup or customisation?

Yes. Wild Apricot is self-service software, but design, data transfer, training and advanced customisation are what we do. That’s where we come in.
