## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, deployed to GitHub Pages (gh-pages branch). Three.js r149 is vendored locally as a classic script (global THREE), so it runs from file:// as well as https. Image textures in WebGL need https; there is a colour-only fallback on file://.

## Users

Two audiences, in this order:

1. **Steven and Helen Jones** (Creative Directors, Highland Creative), plus Reece Jones (Systems Analyst). They will see this in a pitch meeting or by email from The Collaborative Space. Steven founded the studio in 1985; they are experienced designers and will judge craft harshly. The job: decide whether The Collaborative Space is worth partnering with.
2. **Highland Creative's own prospective clients** (the imagined visitors of the redesign concept): Southern Highlands small businesses, professional firms, and clubs/associations/charities looking for a Wild Apricot membership site. The job: trust the studio and get in touch.

## Product Purpose

A pitch deliverable from **The Collaborative Space** to Highland Creative with two parts:

- **Pitch cover:** "Prepared for Highland Creative". A respectful Quick Wins audit of their live site plus the service gaps The Collaborative Space can fill behind the scenes (white-label).
- **Redesign concept:** a multi-page, mostly functional (70%+) modernised version of highlandcreative.com.au built from their real content and photos.

Success: Steven and Helen are impressed enough to book a follow-up conversation or a trial project.

## Positioning

Highland Creative: a family-run Southern Highlands studio since 1985 that does print and web design, and calls itself "Australia's premier Wild Apricot partner". It carries 40 years of hand-drawn-to-digital craft and a published manifesto ("inspired by nature and our surrounds").

The Collaborative Space positions itself as extra hands that complement the studio, not a competitor that replaces it.

## Operating Context

- Shown on a laptop in a meeting, then emailed as a zip.
- The redesign is a concept. It is never published under Highland Creative's name or domain, and The Collaborative Space credit is visible on the cover.
- Contact forms and payments are front-end only (validation and success states, no backend).

## Brand Commitments

- Name "Highland Creative". Taglines in use: "Fresh air, fresh ideas", "graphic & web design that works", "Creative Graphic and Website Design that Works For You", "The Art of Business".
- Seasonal identity: their site rotates spring/summer/autumn/winter Southern Highlands photography.
- Existing logo files: HCreative_Logo_H_369.png, favicon.
- Voice: warm, personal, sincere (see the manifesto).
- The pitch must be respectful: framed as "we noticed while admiring your work", never mocking.
- **Standing rule from The Collaborative Space (binding, all projects):** every page is 3D and alive. Massive transitions, continuous motion, and real 3D that visitors can touch, drag and watch move, across the whole site, not just the hero. A static 2D page is a failed deliverable. A reduced-motion path is still required.

## Product Principles

1. Their truth, better told: every fact, photo and quote is real; only the presentation is new.
2. Respect earns the meeting: the audit reads like a favour from peers.
3. Show, don't pitch: a working, clickable concept beats a list of promises.
4. Proposed means labelled: anything Highland Creative does not offer today is marked as a concept.
